Figure 8. Yearly distribution of the 639 surveyed journals by the percentage of OpenAlex works linked to at least one institution ID, 2020–2025. The solid blue line shows the unweighted mean across journals and the dashed brown line the median; shading shows the 25–75, 10–90 and 5–95 percentile ranges. All document types in the default core corpus are included; journal membership uses primary_location.source.id. Journal-years with no works are excluded from the distributions. Counts were collected on 20 October 2026.

Across the 3,824 journal-year observations with at least one work, the mean share linked to an institution was 91.77%. The annual mean was 91.28% in 2020 and 91.39% in 2025 (change: +0.12 percentage points). The median was 95.65% in 2020 and 96.45% in 2025. This measures the presence of any institutional link, rather than the correctness or completeness of author affiliations.
