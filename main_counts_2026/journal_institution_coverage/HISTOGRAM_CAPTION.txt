Distribution of the surveyed journals by the percentage of OpenAlex works linked to at least one institution ID, separately for 2020–2025. Each panel is a histogram with 10-percentage-point bins and common axes. Bins include their lower boundary and exclude their upper boundary, except the final 90–100% bin, which includes 100%. Each nonempty journal-year contributes one observation. Journal-years with zero works are excluded (0, 1, 1, 2, 3 and 3 respectively). Primary journal source; core corpus; all document types; no institution restriction. Data collected 20 October 2026.
